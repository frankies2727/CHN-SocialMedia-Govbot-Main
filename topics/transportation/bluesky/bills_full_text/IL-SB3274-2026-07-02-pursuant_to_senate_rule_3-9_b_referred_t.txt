Introduced 2/3/2026, by Sen. Terri Bryant

SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED:
Appropriates $250,000 to the Department of Transportation for
expenses relating to the operations of the Ste. Genevieve – Modoc River
Ferry. Effective July 1, 2026.

LRB104 19060 LNS 32505 b

A BILL FOR

LRB104 19060 LNS 32505 b

AN ACT concerning appropriations.

Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois,

represented in the General Assembly:

Section 5. The sum of $250,000, or so much of that sum as

may be necessary, is appropriated from the General Revenue

Fund to the Department of Transportation for expenses related

to the operations of the Ste. Genevieve - Modoc River Ferry.

Section 99. Effective date. This Act takes effect July 1,
2026.
