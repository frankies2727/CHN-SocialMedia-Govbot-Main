Sec. 1051. (1) The bridge on South Grand Avenue over highway I-96 in the village of Fowlerville shall be known as the "LCpl Michael W. Hanks Memorial Bridge". (2) Subject to section 2, a sign designating the location of the "LCpl Michael W. Hanks Memorial Bridge" must be placed at the approach of the bridge on highway I-96. (3) The state transportation department shall coordinate with the Livingston County road commission in placing an appropriate sign designating the location of the "LCpl Michael W. Hanks Memorial Bridge" at each approach of the bridge on South Grand Avenue.
