II119th CONGRESS2d SessionS. 4830IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATESJune 18, 2026Mr. Schatz (for himself and Ms. Hirono) introduced the following bill; which was read twice and referred to the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental AffairsA BILLTo designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 86–014 Farrington Highway in Waianae, Hawaii, as the United States Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building.1.United States Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building(a)DesignationThe facility of the United States Postal Service located at 86–014 Farrington Highway in Waianae, Hawaii, shall be known and designated as the United States Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building.(b)ReferencesAny reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the United States Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building.
