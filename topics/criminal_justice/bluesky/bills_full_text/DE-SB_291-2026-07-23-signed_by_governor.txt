AN ACT TO AMEND TITLE 11 OF THE DELAWARE CODE RELATING TO
REPORTING AND REVIEW OF DEATHS OF INDIVIDUALS IN CUSTODY OF LAW
ENFORCEMENT.

Assumptions:
1. This Act becomes effective upon signature by the Governor.
2. This Act formalizes the requirement that law-enforcement agencies report when someone dies
while in law-enforcement custody and establishes formal compliance reporting among applicable
agencies.
3. The Department of Health and Social Services, Division of Public Health (DPH), anticipates that, for
further compliance, it would require the addition of an “in-custody” checkbox in its Delaware Vital
Events Registration System (DelVERS) to allow for a Medical Examiner to specify whether a death
occurred while the individual was in custody. Further, the addition of this field will ensure more
accurate data collection and provide it to the Statistical Analysis Center.
a. The DPH estimates a one-time implementation cost between $34,107 to $40,817 to complete
this electronic system update.
