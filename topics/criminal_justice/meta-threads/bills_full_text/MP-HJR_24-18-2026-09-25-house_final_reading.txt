A HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION
To approve the Governor's request to exempt and fill essential,
vacant personnel positions within the Department of Corrections,
pursuant to Public Law 24-20, Section 602(a).
WHEREAS, the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth
Legislature enacted Public Law 24-20, also known as the Revised Appropriations
and Budget Authority Act of 2026; and
WHEREAS, Section 602(a) of Public Law 24-20 mandates a freeze on
filling new or vacant positions within the Executive Branch, except for positions
essential to the delivery of public services; and
WHEREAS, Section 602(a) explicitly permits exceptions for other
essential positions as determined by the Governor and formally approved by the
Legislature via a joint resolution; and
WHEREAS, the Department of Corrections bears the critical constitutional
and statutory responsibility of maintaining public safety, institutional security, and
the comprehensive care, custody, and rehabilitation of incarcerated individuals
within the Commonwealth; and

WHEREAS, persistent staffing vacancies within the Department of
Corrections severely impact operational safety, increase mandatory overtime costs,
and compromise the security of both the institutional staff and the general public;
and
WHEREAS, through Governor's Communication 24-170, the Governor
has formally certified to the Legislature that specific vacant personnel positions
within the Department of Corrections are absolutely essential for the continuous
and safe delivery of public services; and
WHEREAS, the Legislature recognizes that stabilizing the workforce of
the Department of Corrections is vital to ensuring safe operational environments
and maintaining compliance with local and federal safety mandates;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the House of
Representatives of the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth
Legislature, the Senate concurring, that the following twenty-one personnel are
essential for the delivery of public service and approved by the Legislature:
1 Parole Program Specialist
20 Correction Cadets
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Speaker of the House of
Representatives and the President of the Senate shall certify and the House Clerk
and the Senate Legislative Secretary shall attest to the adoption of this joint
resolution and thereafter the House Clerk shall transmit a copy to Anthony C.
