State of Illinois
and 2026
Introduced 2/4/2026, by Sen. Javier L. Cervantes

SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED:
Appropriates $2,500,000 from the General Revenue Fund to the
Department of Human Services for a grant to the Illinois Prison Project for
the purposes of legal support, representation, and education for
incarcerated persons in the Illinois Department of Corrections. Effective
July 1, 2026.

A BILL FOR

LRB104 17656 RLC 31087 b

AN ACT concerning appropriations.

Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois,

represented in the General Assembly:

Section 5. The amount of $2,500,000, or so much of that

amount as may be necessary, is appropriated from the General

Revenue Fund to the Department of Human Services for a grant to

the Illinois Prison Project for the purposes of legal support,

representation, and education for incarcerated persons in the

Illinois Department of Corrections.

Section 99. Effective date. This Act takes effect July 1,
2026.
