AN ACT

Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," in school safety and security,
providing for prohibition of artificial intelligence for
school-based mental health and counseling.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1.

The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known

as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a

section to read:

Section 1323-B.

Prohibition of artificial intelligence for

school-based mental health and counseling.
(a)

Prohibition.--Except as otherwise provided in this

section, a school entity may not utilize artificial

intelligence, artificial intelligence chatbots or generative

artificial intelligence, including synthetic content, to provide

a service or experience to a student that would constitute the

practice of a school-based mental health professional.

(b)

Certain limitations on contract.--An artificial

intelligence supplier shall not enter into a contract with a

school entity to provide an artificial intelligence system,

artificial intelligence technology or any generative artificial

intelligence, including synthetic content, that is programmed to

provide a service or experience to a student that would

constitute the practice of a school-based mental health

professional.

(c)

Prohibited uses.--A school entity may not use or provide

the use of an artificial intelligence mental health system to:

(1)

Diagnose the mental health condition of a student.

(2)

Provide psychological or behavioral therapy or

counseling to a student.

(3)

therapeutic intervention to a student.

Provide individualized mental health treatment or

(4)

Determine or recommend an individualized mental

health diagnosis or treatment plan to a student.

(5)

Represent, expressly or by implication, that the

artificial intelligence system is a school psychologist,

school counselor or school social worker.

(6)

Represent, expressly or by implication, that the

artificial intelligence system can substitute for a school

psychologist, school counselor or school social worker.

(7)

Provide a service to a student that would constitute

mental health therapy, psychological therapy or

individualized mental health treatment.

(d)

Construction.--Nothing in this section shall be

construed to:
(1)

prohibit a school-based mental health professional

employed by a school entity from using artificial

intelligence to perform tasks for administrative support,

subject to the requirements of 20 U.S.C. § 1232g (relating to

family educational and privacy rights); or

(2)

supersede or preempt any provisions of a collective

bargaining agreement negotiated by a school entity and an

exclusive representative of the employees in accordance with

the act of July 23, 1970 (P.L.563, No.195), known as the

Public Employe Relations Act.

(e)

Definitions.--As used in this section, the following

words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this

subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:

"Artificial intelligence."
(1)

All of the following:

A machine-based system that can, for a given set of

human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations

or decisions influencing real or virtual environments,

including the ability to:

(i)

perceive real and virtual environments;

(ii)

abstract perceptions made under subparagraph

(i) into models through analysis in an automated manner;

and

(iii)

use model inference to formulate options for

information or action based on outcomes under

subparagraphs (i) and (ii).

(2)

intelligence.

"Artificial intelligence chatbot" or "AI chatbot."

Artificial intelligence technology that uses generative

artificial intelligence to simulate or engage in an interactive

conversation with a user. The term does not include artificial

intelligence technology that provides only scripted, prewritten

The term includes generative artificial

or predetermined output.
"Artificial intelligence technology."

A computer system,

application or other product that uses or incorporates one or

more forms of artificial intelligence.

"Generative artificial intelligence."

The class of

artificial intelligence models that emulate the structure and

characteristics of input data in order to generate synthetic

content.

"Operator."

An individual or entity, including a

corporation, partnership, limited liability company, business

trust, estate, foundation, association, organization or trust,

or an agent or subsidiary thereof, that offers the use of or

provides an AI chatbot to a consumer, if the AI chatbot is

bought from or otherwise provided by a supplier.

"School-based mental health professional."

An individual who

is certified under Article XI and satisfies the department's

requirements for public school employment. The term includes a

school counselor, school social worker or school psychologist as

prescribed in 22 Pa. Code § 49.11(b) (relating to general) or

49.101 (relating to general).

"School entity."

A school district, area career and

technical school, intermediate unit, cyber charter school,

charter school or regional charter school.

"Supplier."

(1)

As follows:

A seller, lessor, assignor, developer, creator,

offeror, broker or other person that regularly solicits,

engages in or enforces transactions with a consumer regarding

an AI chatbot, whether or not the person deals directly with

the consumer.

(2)

The term includes an operator.

"Synthetic content."

Information, including images, videos,

audio clips or text, that has been modified or generated by

algorithms, including by artificial intelligence.

Section 2.

This act shall take effect in 60 days.
