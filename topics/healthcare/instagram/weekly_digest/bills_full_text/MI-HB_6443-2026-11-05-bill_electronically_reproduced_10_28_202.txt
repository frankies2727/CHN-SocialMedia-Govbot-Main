Sec. 21528a. If the results of a medical screening examination

indicate that a patient's emergency medical condition requires the

services of an on-call physician to provide the patient with

treatment necessary to bring the patient to stabilization, the

hospital shall attempt to contact the on-call physician and shall

direct the physician to appear at the hospital within a reasonable

amount of time to bring the patient to stabilization.

Sec. 21528b. If a patient has received a medical screening

examination and the health professional performing the medical

screening examination determines that an emergency medical

condition exists and the patient has not been brought to

stabilization, the hospital shall not transfer the patient to

another hospital unless all of the following conditions are met:

(a) The patient is provided medical treatment within the

hospital's capacity that minimizes any risk to the patient's

health.

(b) The receiving hospital has the space and qualified

personnel available to treat the patient and has agreed to accept

the transfer of the patient and to provide appropriate medical

Sec. 21528c. (1) Except as otherwise provided in this section,

if a patient has received a medical screening examination and the

health professional examining the patient determines that an

emergency medical condition exists, the hospital shall not

discharge the patient unless the patient has been brought to

stabilization.

(2) If a patient described in subsection (1) has not been

brought to stabilization, the patient may be discharged only if

both of the following apply:

(a) After being informed of the hospital's obligations under

this section and the risk of discharge, the patient or the

patient's representative requests a discharge in writing.

(b) The hospital offers the patient further medical

examination and treatment and informs the patient or the patient's

representative of the risks and benefits of the examination and

treatment but the patient or the patient's representative does not

consent to the medical examination and treatment.

(3) If a patient or the patient's representative does not

consent to medical examination or treatment under subsection (2),

the patient's medical record must contain a description of the

examination and, if applicable, the treatment and a statement that

the patient or the patient's representative refused medical

examination or treatment. The hospital shall take all reasonable

steps to secure the patient's written informed refusal, which must

indicate that the patient has been informed of the risks and

benefits of the examination and treatment, if applicable.

Sec. 21528d. A hospital shall ensure that a physician employed

by or under contract to the hospital does not do any of the

following:

(a) Sign a certification under section 21528b that states the

medical benefits reasonably expected from appropriate treatment at

another hospital outweigh the increased risks to the patient from

being transferred if the physician knows or should have known the

benefits did not outweigh the risks.

(b) Misrepresents a patient's condition or other information

under section 21528b, including a hospital's obligation under that

section.

(c) If the physician is the on-call physician, fails or

refuses to present to the hospital within a reasonable period of

time after being contacted under section 21528a.
