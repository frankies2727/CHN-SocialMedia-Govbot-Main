AN ACT

Providing for safety regarding artificial intelligence in
companionship applications; and imposing a penalty.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania

hereby enacts as follows:

Section 1.

Short title.

This act shall be known and may be cited as the Artificial

Intelligence in Companionship Applications Safety Act.

Section 2.

Definitions.

The following words and phrases when used in this act shall

have the meanings given to them in this section unless the

context clearly indicates otherwise:

"AI companion."
(1)

A system that:
(i)

uses artificial intelligence and generative

artificial intelligence to simulate a human or humanlike

relationship with emotional recognition algorithms; and

(ii)

interacts with a user by compiling previous

information or discussions from user sessions to:

(A)

engage with the user's preferences;

(B)

personalize interaction based on user

preferences;

(C)

order to illicit feelings; and

(D)

ask emotion-based questions unprompted in

maintain conversations to the user feelings

or personal matters.
(2)

The term does not include AI used for customer

service, research, technical assistance or systems for

employee productivity in the workplace.

"AI model."

A component of an informational system program

that uses artificial intelligence for computational techniques

to produce outputs from a given set of outputs.

"Artificial intelligence" or "AI."

A machine-based system

that can, for a given set of human-defined objectives, make

predictions, recommendations or decisions influencing real or

virtual environments, including the ability to:

(1)

perceive real and virtual environments;

(2)

abstract perceptions made under paragraph (1) into

AI models through analysis in an automated manner; and
(3)

use AI model inference to formulate options for

information or action based on outcomes under paragraphs (1)

and (2).

"Emotional recognition algorithms."

Artificial intelligence

that detects and reacts to an individual user's emotions from

collected data like text, voice audio or video.

"Generative artificial intelligence."

The class of AI models

that emulate the structure and characteristics of input data in

order to generate derived synthetic content.

"Operator."

Any individual, association, business, member or

subsidiary who operates for or provides an AI companion to a

user.

"Self-harm."

"Suicidal ideation."

Intentional self-injury.
A feeling, thought or consideration

with the preoccupation of the idea of death or suicide.
"Synthetic content."

Information, such as images, videos,

audio clips or text, that have been significantly modified or

generated by algorithms, including by artificial intelligence.

"User."

(1)

An individual who:
uses an AI companion for personal use within this

Commonwealth; and

(2)

Artificial intelligence companion.

Certain protocols required.--It shall be unlawful for an

operator to provide an AI companion to a user unless the AI

companion contains protocols that:

(1)
harm;

(2)

(3)

ideation or expressions of self-harm are recognized.

(b)

Referral to crisis center.--The referral required under

subsection (a)(3) shall include:
(1)

crisis service contact information, including the

Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, or a subsequent iteration;

(2)

user; or

(3)

the closest behavioral health crisis centers to the

other appropriate crisis services.

(c)

Prohibition.--An AI companion may not claim, imply or

advertise that the AI companion is a licensed emotional support

professional or mental health professional or replaces services

rendered by a licensed mental health professional.

Section 4.

Artificial intelligence companion requirements.

An operator shall:
(1)

Publish details on the protocol on the operator's

Internet website.
(2)

At the beginning of a session with an AI companion

and once every three hours during the session, provide a

notification to the user stating, either verbally or in

writing, that the user is communicating with an AI companion

and not a human.

Section 5.

Violations and penalty.

A user may file a complaint with the Attorney General

alleging that an operator has violated this act, to which the

following shall apply:

(1)

The Attorney General, if provided satisfactory

evidence that an operator has, or intends to, violate this

act, can bring an action in the name and on behalf of the

people of this Commonwealth against the operator.

(2)

subject to:

(i)

An operator in violation of this act shall be

a civil penalty of no more than $15,000 per day

per violation; and

(ii)

additional remedies that the court deems

appropriate.

(3)

The Attorney General may initiate, in Commonwealth

Court or the court of common pleas of the county in which the

individual or entity resides, an action in equity for an

injunction to restrain a violation of this act, to which the

following shall apply:

(i)

The injunction may be issued by a court without

requiring proof that an individual has been injured or

experienced harm.

(ii)

The respondent shall comply with the injunction

within five days.
Section 6.

Effective date.

This act shall take effect in 60 days.
