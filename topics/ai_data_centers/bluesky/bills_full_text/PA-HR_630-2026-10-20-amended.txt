THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIA

HOUSE RESOLUTION
No. 630

HOWARD, GUZMAN, SANCHEZ, SHUSTERMAN, DONAHUE AND PASHINSKI,
SEPTEMBER 23, 2026
REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON ENERGY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026
A RESOLUTION

Directing the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to
conduct a study on data center decommissioning in
Pennsylvania and provide recommendations.
WHEREAS, Data centers are rooms, buildings or facilities that

serve to contain and house essential applications and data for

the information technology economy, which allows individuals to

access various digital services and applications on the

Internet; and

WHEREAS, Data centers have become increasingly important to

meeting society's Internet demands, much of which is due to the

growth of artificial intelligence services; and

WHEREAS, Pennsylvania is currently home to 174 data centers,

with the greater Pittsburgh area serving as home to 36 data

centers, more than any other market in the Commonwealth; and

WHEREAS, While many data centers in the United States were

built recently, all data centers eventually reach the end of

their operational cycle, at which point these facilities must be

decommissioned, a critical process for data centers; and

WHEREAS, The decommissioning process for data centers

includes tasks such as removing all equipment, sanitization of

data to prevent data recovery, environmental disposal of

hardware and resale of reusable assets; and

WHEREAS, If this process is not done properly and carefully,

it can lead to issues such as data breaches and environmental

hazards; and

WHEREAS, Some local government units in Pennsylvania have

enacted ordinances to require data center owners to provide

decommissioning plans and financial assurance for their

decommissioning process prior to beginning construction for the

data center, but no Statewide requirement for such plans or

assurances currently exists; and

WHEREAS, This creates a risk that municipalities and

taxpayers could be forced to pay for the decommissioning of data

centers if their local government units do not require

decommissioning plans and financial assurances before a data

center begins operation; and

WHEREAS, It is in the public interest that Pennsylvania

residents realize the benefits of data centers without being

left to implement and pay for their eventual decommissioning;

therefore be it

RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives direct the

Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to conduct a study on

data center decommissioning in Pennsylvania and provide

recommendations; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee,
in conducting the study, examine:
(1)

the costs typically required to decommission a data

center, both in the Commonwealth and the United States;

(2)

ordinances enacted in this Commonwealth and other

states that require a decommissioning plan and ongoing

financial assurance for a data center prior to the

commencement of construction or operation of the data center;

(3)

ordinances enacted in this Commonwealth and other

states regarding the decommissioning of warehouses and

similar structures or any decommissioning plan or financial

assurance requirements for the decommissioning of such

structures;

(4)

cases in which individuals apart from a data center

owner or operator have been made to cover the costs of data

center decommissioning in this Commonwealth, if applicable;

and

(5)

expected decommissioning dates of data centers

currently operating in this Commonwealth;
and be it further
RESOLVED, That the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee

issue a report with its findings and recommendations to the

General Assembly within three months of the adoption of this

resolution.
