WILLIAMS AND FLEMING, JULY 20, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, JULY 20, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing July 25, 2026, as "World IVF Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, In vitro fertilization, or IVF, is a series of medical procedures designed to help individuals or couples experiencing infertility issues get pregnant; andWHEREAS, The IVF process involves the extraction of eggs from the ovaries of the patient or donor, the fertilization of the eggs in a petri dish using sperm from a partner or donor and the transfer of the fertilized eggs to the uterus of the patient or a gestational carrier; andWHEREAS, Embryologists are necessary for the IVF process and all infertility treatments, as embryologists are the ones who monitor the fertilized eggs to ensure that they are healthy before transferring to the uterus; andWHEREAS, Embryologists also perform genetic testing to identify any potential genetic disorders that may be present in the fertilized egg, and embryologists can also perform Preimplantation Genetic Testing to determine if there are 1234567891011121314151617 genetic mutations or chromosomal abnormalities present that may be the cause of miscarriage or infertility issues; andWHEREAS, Since the first baby to be successfully born from IVF on July 25, 1978, more than 10 million babies have been born using IVF worldwide, with more than 500,000 new IVF births each year; andWHEREAS, In the United States, roughly 3% of all births each year are the result of IVF; andWHEREAS, IVF often serves as the only chance for those experiencing fallopian tube damage, ovulation disorders, male factor infertility, recurrent miscarriages, genetic problems and other infertility issues to become pregnant and grow their family; andWHEREAS, The IVF process is not without risks, as the procedures not only carry a high financial cost, with each IVF cycle costing on average more than $15,000, but it also increases the odds of developing certain health complications, such as ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome or twisting of the ovary or fallopian tubes; andWHEREAS, There is also the risk of pregnancy complications, such as multiple pregnancies and ectopic pregnancies as well as low birth weight and premature birth; andWHEREAS, Despite the potential risks, access to IVF is crucial for couples who may not be able to conceive without the process;
