HOUSE SUBSTITUTE FOR
SENATE BILL NO. 415

A bill to amend 1939 PA 280, entitled
"The social welfare act,"
(MCL 400.1 to 400.119b) by adding section 109t.
THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF MICHIGAN ENACT:

Sec. 109t. Beginning on the effective date of the amendatory

act that added this section, the department shall provide coverage

under the medical assistance program for group prenatal care

services. As used in this section:

(a) "Group prenatal care services" means not more than 12

prenatal care visits provided in a group setting that are based on

an evidence-based model.

(b) "Group setting" means a group of not less than 3
individuals.
