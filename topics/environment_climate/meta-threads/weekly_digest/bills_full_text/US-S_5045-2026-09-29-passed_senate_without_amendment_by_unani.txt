and(CC)is an event caused by human activity that is unlikely to recur at a particular location or a natural event; or(bb)that is a prescribed fire; and(II)that is—(aa)determined by the Administrator, through the process established in the regulations revised under paragraph (2)(B), to be an exceptional event;
