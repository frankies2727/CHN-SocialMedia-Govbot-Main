STATEMENT This bill, which is designated as the Affordable Power Purchase Agreements Extension Act, amends current law regarding renewable energy power purchase agreements (energy contracts).  Under current law, certain public entities are authorized to enter into energy contracts for a term not to exceed 15 years.  The bill authorizes certain public entities to extend, without public advertising for bids and bidding, existing energy contracts for a term not to exceed 30 years in the aggregate. It is the sponsors intent that amending current law to allow certain energy contracts to be extended, subject to safeguards that preserve the ratepayer benefits of the original agreements, is in the public interest.
