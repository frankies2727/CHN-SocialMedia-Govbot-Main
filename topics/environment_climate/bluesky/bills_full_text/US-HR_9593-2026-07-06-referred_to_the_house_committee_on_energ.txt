SEC. 2. PROHIBITION AGAINST FOOD PACKAGING CON-

TAINING INTENTIONALLY ADDED PFAS.

(a) IN GENERAL.—Section 301 of the Federal Food,

Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 331) is amended by
adding at the end the following:

‘‘(jjj)(1) The introduction or delivery for introduction

into interstate commerce of food packaging containing in8 tentionally added PFAS.

‘‘(2) In this paragraph, the term ‘PFAS’ means a

class of fluorinated organic chemicals containing at least
one fully fluorinated methyl or methylene carbon atom.’’.

(b) APPLICABILITY.—The amendment made by sub-

section (a) applies beginning on January 1, 2027.
