An Act establishing a temporary moratorium on large scale photovoltaic installations and battery
energy storage systems (BESS) in the town of Becket.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:

SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, and
notwithstanding any other provision of the Zoning By-laws to the contrary, the Town is
authorized to adopt and impose a temporary moratorium on the use of land or structures for
Large-Scale Solar Photovoltaic Installations and BESS other than BESS accessory to individual
residential use.  The moratorium shall be in effect for six (6) months following its adoption or the
date on which the Town adopts amendments to the Zoning By-laws concerning Large-Scale
Solar Photovoltaic Installations and BESS, whichever occurs earlier. During the moratorium
period, the Town shall undertake a planning process to study, review, analyze, and address what
revisions to the Zoning By-laws relative to Large-Scale Solar Photovoltaic Installations and
BESS are needed or desirable to allow for and regulate such use consistent with applicable law
while protecting the Town's ecological resources and furthering its planning goals.

of 2 12  SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
