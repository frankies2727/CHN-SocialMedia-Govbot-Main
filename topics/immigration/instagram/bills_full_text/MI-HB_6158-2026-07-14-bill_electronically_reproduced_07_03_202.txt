HOUSE BILL NO. 6158

July 03, 2026, Introduced by Reps. Miller, B. Carter, McKinney, Wooden, Paiz, Rheingans and
Myers-Phillips and referred to Committee on Government Operations.
A bill to prohibit governmental entities from allowing access
to certain nonpublic areas under certain circumstances; and to
provide for the powers and duties of certain state and local
governmental officers and entities.
THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF MICHIGAN ENACT:

Sec. 1. This act may be cited as the "safe access to
facilities act".

Sec. 2. As used in this act:

(a) "Federal immigration authority" means a federal

immigration officer.

(b) "Governmental entity" means a state department or agency
or a local unit of government.

(c) "Judicial warrant" means a warrant issued by a court that
is based on probable cause.

(d) "Nonpublic area" means an area not open to the public.

Sec. 3. A governmental entity shall not knowingly grant a

federal immigration authority access to a nonpublic area without a

judicial warrant.

Sec. 4. (1) This act does not do any of the following:

(a) Restrict a criminal investigation.

(b) Interfere with the execution of a judicial warrant.

(c) Limit cooperation in a criminal matter.

(d) Restrict actions necessary to address an immediate threat

to public safety.

(e) Delay, restrict, or interfere with access necessary to

provide emergency medical care or to protect the immediate health

and safety of an individual.

(f) Prohibit or restrict lawful federal enforcement actions.

(2) This act governs only the conduct of state and local

governmental entities and employees.
