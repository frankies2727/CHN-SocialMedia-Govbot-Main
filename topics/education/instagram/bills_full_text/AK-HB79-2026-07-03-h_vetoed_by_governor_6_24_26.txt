A BILL
FOR AN ACT ENTITLED

"An Act naming the Vic Fischer Shoup Bay State Marine Park."

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF ALASKA:

* Section 1. AS 41.21.304 is amended by adding a new subsection to read:

(b) The marine park unit of the Alaska state park system designated in (a)(4)
of this section is named Vic Fischer Shoup Bay State Marine Park.
