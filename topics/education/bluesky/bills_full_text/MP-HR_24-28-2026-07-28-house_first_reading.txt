A HOUSE RESOLUTION
To recognize, honor, and express appreciation to Rufina Tuloi Borja
Seman for 50 years of exemplary and distinguished service in
education.
WHEREAS, the Legislature recognizes that selfless service and steadfast
dedication to educating and uplifting our youth—exemplified by those who devote
their lives to teaching, mentoring, and fostering academic and personal growth-
serve as a guiding light, inspiring each of us to cultivate our talents and contribute
wholeheartedly to the prosperity of the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana
Islands (CNMI); and
WHEREAS, Rufina Tuloi Borja Seman has devoted 50 years to the noble
profession of education, demonstrating unwavering commitment to the intellectual,
social, and emotional growth of countless students; and
WHEREAS, she began her distinguished career with 5 years of service at
Mans Stella Elementary School in Palau, where she established a strong foundation
for young learners and inspired a lifelong love of learning; and

WHEREAS, she continued her educational journey with 13 years at Mount
Carmel School in Saipan, shaping students' minds and character through
dedication, compassion, and exemplary teaching; and
WHEREAS, she contributed 4 years to early childhood education through
the Head Start program in Saipan, nurturing children during their most formative
years and promoting school readiness; and
WHEREAS, she has served 19 years, and continues to serve with
distinction at San Vicente Elementary School in Saipan, where she remains a
steadfast mentor, role model, and pillar of the school community; and
WHEREAS, throughout her career, Rufina Tuloi Borja Seman has
exemplified professionalism, patience, and a profound passion for teaching, leaving
an enduring legacy in every community she has served; and
WHEREAS, her dedication has significantly advanced the educational
attainment and well-being of generations of students, their families, and the broader
community; and
WHEREAS, this August Body is honored and privileged to recognize,
commend, and express appreciation to Rufina Tuloi Borja Seman for 50 years of
exemplary and distinguished service in education; and
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the House of
Representatives of the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth
Legislature, that the House hereby honor and commend Rufina Tuloi Borja Seman
