SEC. 2. REALLOCATION OF VOUCHER FUNDING.

Section 8(o) of the United States Housing Act of

1937 is amended by adding at the end the following:

‘‘(24) REALLOCATION OF AMOUNTS.—The Secretary shall, at the end of each fiscal year—

‘‘(A) recapture from each public housing

agency any amounts provided to such public

housing agency for tenant-based assistance

under paragraph (1)(A), not including any ad-

ministrative fees or other amounts made avail-

able under subsection (q), that such public

housing agency did not obligate during such fis-

cal year; and

‘‘(B) provide amounts recaptured under

subparagraph (A) to public housing agencies

that used all of the amounts provided to them

for tenant-based assistance under paragraph

(1)(A).’’.
